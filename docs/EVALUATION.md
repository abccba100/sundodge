# Evaluation

Generated by `npm run evaluate` (scripts/evaluate.ts) on the bundled OpenStreetMap areas. Seed 20261017, 200 random walks per area, α = 3. All numbers are model output on OSM data: they measure minutes in direct sun as SunDodge computes it, not measured temperatures or health effects.

Method:

- Walks: random pairs of street nodes 500–1,500 m apart in a straight line, both at least 150 m inside the area edge.
- For each walk and departure time, SunDodge finds the shortest route and the SunDodge route, with shadows at mid-walk, exactly as the app does.
- "Open streets only" re-runs the SunDodge route with covered ways (arcades, covered alleys, underground and indoor passages) removed, to show how much of the saving depends on them.
- "When to leave": the best departure within ±60 min (10-min steps) compared with leaving at 15:00, on the SunDodge route.
- Medians with the interquartile range in brackets.

## Limits

- Building heights: where OSM has no height or floor count, the height is estimated from the building type (most of the Seoul area). Results there are less certain.
- Wide roads are single centre lines in OSM. Sampling the centre of a wide road can overstate the sun on a sidewalk next to buildings, for the shortest route more than for the SunDodge route.
- Shadows are evaluated at the middle of each walk; the shadows moving during the walk are not modelled.
- "Minutes in direct sun" is an exposure measure, not a heat-stress or health score.
